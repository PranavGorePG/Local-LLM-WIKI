The University of Toronto is an academic institution that has researchers involved in the field of machine learning and artificial intelligence. Aidan N. Gomez, one of the authors of the "Attention Is All You Need" paper, was affiliated with the University of Toronto during his work on the Transformer model, highlighting the collaboration between academic research and industry labs.
