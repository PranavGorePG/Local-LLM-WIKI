NIPS 2017 refers to the 31st Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems, held in Long Beach, CA, USA. The seminal paper "Attention Is All You Need", which introduced the Transformer model, was presented at this conference. This venue is significant as it marks the official introduction of a model that would profoundly impact the field of sequence modeling and natural language processing.
