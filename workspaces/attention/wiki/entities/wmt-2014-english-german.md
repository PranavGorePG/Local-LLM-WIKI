The WMT 2014 English-German dataset is a benchmark dataset used for evaluating machine translation systems. It consists of approximately 4.5 million sentence pairs. This dataset was used in the "Attention Is All You Need" paper to train and evaluate the Transformer model, which achieved a BLEU score of 28.4 on this task. The dataset utilizes byte-pair encoding for tokenization, with a shared source-target vocabulary of around 37,000 tokens.
