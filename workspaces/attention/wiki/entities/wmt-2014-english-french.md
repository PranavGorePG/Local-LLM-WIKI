The WMT 2014 English-French dataset is a large-scale benchmark for machine translation. It comprises 36 million sentences, with tokens split into a 32,000 word-piece vocabulary. In the "Attention Is All You Need" paper, the Transformer model trained on this dataset achieved a state-of-the-art BLEU score of 41.0 after 3.5 days of training on eight GPUs. This dataset is crucial for evaluating the performance of sequence transduction models on complex translation tasks.
