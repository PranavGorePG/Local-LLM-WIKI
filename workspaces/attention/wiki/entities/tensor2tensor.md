Tensor2Tensor is a software library developed by Google Brain for deep learning research. It is primarily used for sequence modeling tasks and is built on top of TensorFlow. The library was instrumental in the development of the Transformer model, as it provided the codebase for training and evaluating the Transformer architecture. Its flexibility and efficiency allowed researchers to experiment with various model configurations and hyperparameters, leading to significant advancements in the field of natural language processing and machine translation.
