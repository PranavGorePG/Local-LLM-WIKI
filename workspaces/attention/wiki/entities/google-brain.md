Google Brain is a machine intelligence research project at Google that focuses on deep learning and related fields. It is known for significant contributions to artificial intelligence research and the development of cutting-edge AI models and technologies.

## Overview
Google Brain is a prominent research division within Google that has been instrumental in advancing the field of deep learning. The team comprises researchers and engineers working on a wide range of AI topics, from foundational research to practical applications.

## Key Contributions
The paper "Attention Is All You Need" lists Google Brain as the affiliation for several of its authors, including Ashish Vaswani and Noam Shazeer. This indicates Google Brain's significant involvement in the development of the Transformer model, a foundational architecture in modern NLP and deep learning.

## Role in Context
As a leading AI research organization, Google Brain has been a powerhouse for innovation, contributing numerous pivotal papers and models that shape the direction of AI research. Its work, including the development of the Transformer, has had a profound impact on the broader field.
