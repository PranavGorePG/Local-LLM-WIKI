Google Research is an entity involved in the development of advanced machine learning models. Researchers from Google Research, including authors of the "Attention Is All You Need" paper, contributed to the creation of the Transformer model. This entity has a significant role in pushing the boundaries of sequence transduction and attention-based architectures in natural language processing.
