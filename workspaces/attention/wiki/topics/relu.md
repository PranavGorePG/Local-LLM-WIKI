[[ReLU]] (Rectified Linear Unit) is a popular [[non-linear activation function]] used in deep learning models. It outputs the input directly if it is positive, and zero otherwise. ReLU is often used in [[feedforward neural network layer]]s within the [[Transformer architecture]] and can also be applied in [[Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)|RNN]] computations.
