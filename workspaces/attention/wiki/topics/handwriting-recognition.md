[[Handwriting Recognition]] is the task of converting handwritten text into machine-readable text. This involves processing sequences of pen strokes or images of handwriting. [[Recurrent Neural Network (RNN|RNN]]s have been successfully applied to this field due to their ability to handle sequential input.
