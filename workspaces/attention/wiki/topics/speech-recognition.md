[[Speech Recognition]] is the field of artificial intelligence that enables computers to understand and transcribe spoken language into text. [[Recurrent Neural Network (RNN|RNN]]s and [[Transformer architecture|Transformers]] have been instrumental in advancing the accuracy of speech recognition systems, processing audio waveform sequences to identify phonemes or words.
