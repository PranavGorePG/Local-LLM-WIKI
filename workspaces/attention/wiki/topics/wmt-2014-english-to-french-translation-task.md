The WMT 2014 English-to-French translation task is a benchmark dataset used for evaluating machine translation systems. The "Attention Is All You Need" paper specifically mentions achieving a BLEU score of 41.0 on this task with their proposed Transformer model, which was a new single-model state-of-the-art at the time. This achievement was notable for requiring significantly less training time compared to previous best models.

This dataset is part of the Workshop on Machine Translation (WMT) and is a standard evaluation for English to French language pairs. The Transformer model's performance on this task demonstrated the effectiveness of attention-based architectures over traditional recurrent and convolutional networks for sequence transduction problems.
