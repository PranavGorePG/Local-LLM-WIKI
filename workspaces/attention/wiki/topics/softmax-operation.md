The [[softmax operation]] is a function that converts a vector of real numbers into a [[probability distribution]]. In [[Self-attention]], softmax is applied to the scaled [[attention score]]s to obtain normalized attention weights, which represent the importance of each token's [[Value (V)|Value]] vector in the final output.
