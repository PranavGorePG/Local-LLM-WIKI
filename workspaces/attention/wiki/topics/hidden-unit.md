A [[hidden unit]] is a computational node within the hidden layer(s) of a neural network. In [[Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)|RNN]]s, the outputs of hidden units at one [[time step]] are fed back as inputs to the hidden layer at the next time step, contributing to the network's [[hidden state]] and its ability to process sequential information.
