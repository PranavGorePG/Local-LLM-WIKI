[[GPT]] (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) refers to a series of [[Transformer architecture|Transformer]]-based models developed by OpenAI, renowned for their capabilities in [[Text generation]] and [[Language modeling]]. GPT models are trained on massive datasets and utilize a decoder-only architecture, making them highly effective at producing coherent and contextually relevant human-like text. [[Large Language Model (LLM)]]s like GPT-3 and GPT-4 are prominent examples.
