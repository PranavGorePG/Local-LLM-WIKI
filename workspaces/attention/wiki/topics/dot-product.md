The [[dot product]] is a fundamental operation in linear algebra that takes two vectors and returns a single scalar value. In [[Self-attention]], the dot product between [[Query (Q)|Query]] and [[Key (K)|Key]] vectors is a common method for computing [[attention score]]s, measuring their similarity or alignment.
