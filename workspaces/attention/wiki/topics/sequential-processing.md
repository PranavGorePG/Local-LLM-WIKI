[[Sequential processing]] involves handling data elements one after another, in a specific order. [[Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)|RNN]]s inherently operate through sequential processing, where the output of one step is fed into the next. This contrasts with [[Self-attention]], which enables [[parallelization]] by processing all tokens simultaneously.
