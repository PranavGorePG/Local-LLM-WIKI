In the context of [[Self-attention]], the [[Query (Q)|Query]] vector is derived from an input token's [[embedding]]. It represents what the token is "looking for" in other tokens within the sequence to understand its context. The Query vector is used in calculating [[attention score]]s by interacting with [[Key (K)|Key]] vectors of other tokens.
