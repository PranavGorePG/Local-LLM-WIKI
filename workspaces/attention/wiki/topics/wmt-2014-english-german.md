The WMT 2014 English-to-German translation task is a benchmark dataset and evaluation task used in the field of machine translation. It consists of a large collection of sentence pairs in English and German, used for training and evaluating translation models. This specific task was one of the primary benchmarks used in the "Attention Is All You Need" paper to demonstrate the superior performance of the Transformer model.

Results on this task are typically measured using metrics like the BLEU score. The Transformer model achieved a state-of-the-art BLEU score of 28.4 on this task, significantly outperforming previous leading models and ensembles, and doing so with much greater training efficiency.
