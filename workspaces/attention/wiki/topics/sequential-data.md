[[Sequential data]] is data in which the order of elements is meaningful and carries important information. Examples include time series, text, and audio signals. [[Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)|RNN]]s are fundamentally designed for sequential data, while [[Self-attention]] mechanisms can also process sequential data effectively, often augmented with [[positional encoding]].
