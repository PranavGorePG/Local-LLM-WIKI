In the context of [[Self-attention]], the [[Key (K)|Key]] vector is derived from an input token's [[embedding]]. It represents what the token "offers" or contains in terms of information. Key vectors are compared against [[Query (Q)|Query]] vectors from other tokens to compute [[attention score]]s, determining how much attention each token should pay to others.
