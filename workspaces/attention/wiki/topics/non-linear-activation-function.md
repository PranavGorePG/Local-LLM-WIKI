A [[non-linear activation function]] is applied to the output of a neuron or a layer in a neural network to introduce non-linearity into the model. This is crucial for learning complex patterns. Common examples include [[ReLU]] and tanh. In [[Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)|RNN]]s, activation functions like tanh or ReLU are used when updating the [[hidden state]].
