The [[Transformer]] is a groundbreaking model architecture introduced in "Attention Is All You Need." It relies entirely on [[Self-attention]] mechanisms, abandoning recurrence and convolution for sequence modeling tasks. Its ability to process sequences in parallel and effectively capture long-range dependencies has made it the dominant architecture for many NLP tasks and the basis for models like [[BERT]] and [[GPT]].
