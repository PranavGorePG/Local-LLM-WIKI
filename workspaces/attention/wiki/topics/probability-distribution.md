A [[probability distribution]] is a function that describes the likelihood of obtaining different possible outcomes. In [[Self-attention]], the [[softmax operation]] is used to convert attention scores into a probability distribution, where each value represents the weight assigned to a corresponding [[Value (V)|Value]] vector.
