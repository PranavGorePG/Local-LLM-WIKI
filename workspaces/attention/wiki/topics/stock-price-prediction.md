[[Stock price prediction]] is an application within [[Time Series Forecasting]] that aims to forecast the future price movements of stocks. Models like [[Recurrent Neural Network (RNN|RNN]]s (especially LSTMs) and [[Transformer architecture|Transformers]] are employed to analyze historical price data and identify patterns that might predict future trends.
