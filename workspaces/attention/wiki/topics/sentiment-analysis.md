[[Sentiment analysis]] is an NLP task focused on determining the emotional tone or subjective opinion expressed in a piece of text. This can range from classifying text as positive, negative, or neutral. [[Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)|RNN]]s and [[Transformer architecture|Transformers]] are often employed for sentiment analysis due to their ability to process and understand textual context.
