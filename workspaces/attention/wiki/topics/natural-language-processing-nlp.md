[[Natural Language Processing (NLP)]] is a subfield of [[machine learning model|machine learning]] and artificial intelligence focused on enabling computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language. Common NLP tasks include [[Language modeling]], [[Sentiment analysis]], [[Text generation]], and [[Machine Translation]]. Architectures like [[Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)|RNN]]s and [[Transformer architecture|Transformers]] (which use [[Self-attention]]) are foundational in modern NLP.
