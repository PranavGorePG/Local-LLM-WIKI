[[Attention Is All You Need]]" is a seminal paper by Ashish Vaswani et al., presented at NIPS 2017. It introduces the [[Transformer Architecture]], a novel sequence transduction model that entirely abandons recurrent and convolutional layers, relying exclusively on [[Attention Mechanism]]s. The paper demonstrates that Transformers achieve superior quality and parallelization capabilities in [[Machine Translation]] tasks (e.g., English-to-German and English-to-French), setting new state-of-the-art [[BLEU scores]] with significantly reduced training costs. Key architectural components detailed include [[Self-Attention]], [[Scaled Dot-Product Attention]], and [[Multi-Head Attention]], which allows the model to jointly attend to information from different representation subspaces. To account for sequence order in the absence of recurrence, the Transformer incorporates [[Positional Encoding]]. The paper also highlights the computational advantages of self-attention, particularly in reducing the path length for learning long-range dependencies compared to traditional [[Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)]] and [[Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)]]. This work has profoundly influenced the field of natural language processing and beyond, leading to the development of modern [[Large Language Models (LLMs)]].
