A [[Large Language Model (LLM)]] is a type of [[machine learning model]] characterized by its massive size (billions of parameters) and its training on vast amounts of text data. LLMs are typically based on the [[Transformer architecture]] and excel at a wide range of [[Natural Language Processing (NLP)]] tasks, including [[Text generation]], [[Language modeling]], and [[Machine Translation]]. Examples include models like [[BERT]] and [[GPT]].
