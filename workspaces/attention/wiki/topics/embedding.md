An [[embedding]] is a learned representation of a discrete variable, such as a word or token, typically as a dense vector in a lower-dimensional space. In models like [[Self-attention]] and [[Transformer architecture|Transformers]], input tokens are first converted into embeddings. These embeddings are then transformed into Query, Key, and Value vectors for the attention mechanism.
