[[Language modeling]] is a core task in [[Natural Language Processing (NLP)]] that involves predicting the probability of a sequence of words. Models learn to assign higher probabilities to grammatically correct and semantically meaningful sentences. [[Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)|RNN]]s and [[Transformer architecture|Transformers]] are commonly used for language modeling.
