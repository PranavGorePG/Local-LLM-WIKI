[[Text generation]] is an NLP task focused on creating human-like text. This can range from completing sentences to generating entire articles or stories. [[Recurrent Neural Network (RNN|RNN]]s (especially LSTMs and GRUs) and [[Transformer architecture|Transformers]] (like GPT models) are capable of performing sophisticated text generation.
