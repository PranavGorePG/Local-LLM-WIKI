This wiki serves as a comprehensive knowledge base for concepts related to advanced neural network architectures, primarily focusing on topics like [[Attention Mechanism]]s, [[Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)]], and the [[Transformer Architecture]]. It aims to provide detailed explanations of core components such as [[Self-Attention]], [[Multi-Head Attention]], [[Scaled Dot-Product Attention]], and [[Positional Encoding]], as well as performance metrics like the [[BLEU Score]].Recently, the scope has expanded to include the analysis of [[Large Language Models (LLMs)]] and their interactions with diverse data types, particularly [[Graph-structured data]]. This includes exploring the challenges LLMs face in modeling graph topologies and the emergence of phenomena such as "[[Attention Sink]]" and "[[Skewed Line Sink]]", as well as the role of the [[Attention Window]] in optimizing performance on such data, as investigated in "[[Attention Mechanisms Perspective: Exploring LLM Processing of Graph-Structured Data (Guan et al. 2025)]]". The content is synthesized from various authoritative sources, including seminal papers like "[[NIPS 2017 Attention Is All You Need Paper]]", to offer a clear and interconnected understanding of these complex topics.
