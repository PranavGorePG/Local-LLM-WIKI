The [[BLEU Score]] (Bilingual Evaluation Understudy) is an algorithm for evaluating the quality of text which has been machine-translated from one natural language to another. It measures the correspondence between a machine's output and a human reference translation. A perfect match between a machine translation and a reference translation would yield a score of 1.0 (or 100%). BLEU scores are widely used in [[Machine Translation]] research to compare the performance of different models. The higher the BLEU score, the closer the machine translation is to a professional human translation. Models like the [[Transformer Architecture]], as demonstrated in "[[Attention Is All You Need]]" (Vaswani et al., 2017), aim to achieve and have achieved new state-of-the-art BLEU scores on challenging translation tasks.
