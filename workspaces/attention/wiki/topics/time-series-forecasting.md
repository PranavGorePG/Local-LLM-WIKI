[[Time Series Forecasting]] involves predicting future values based on historical data points collected over time. [[Recurrent Neural Network (RNN|RNN]]s, particularly LSTMs and GRUs, have been widely used for tasks like [[Stock price prediction]] and [[Weather modeling]] due to their ability to model temporal dependencies. [[Transformer architecture|Transformers]] are also increasingly applied to time series problems.
