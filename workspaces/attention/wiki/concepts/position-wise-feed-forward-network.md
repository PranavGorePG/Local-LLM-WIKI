A Position-wise Feed-Forward Network (FFN) is a component within the Transformer architecture that is applied independently and identically to each position in the sequence. Each layer in the encoder and decoder of the Transformer consists of a multi-head attention sub-layer followed by a position-wise FFN.

This FFN typically consists of two linear transformations with a ReLU activation in between. The "Attention Is All You Need" paper specifies that for each layer, the input and output dimensions are $d_{model}=512$, while the inner-layer has a dimensionality of $d_{ff}=2048$. Although the linear transformations are the same across different positions, they utilize distinct parameters from layer to layer. This component plays a crucial role in processing the representations generated by the attention mechanisms.
